Vault-Talk: third-party tools and material

Vault-Talk is a free fan project. The items below are NOT ours. We do not own them and claim no rights
over them. They are downloaded or included only to save players the trouble of finding them, and they
remain the property of their owners. If you are an owner and want something removed, open an issue on
this repository and it will be taken down.

FaceFXWrapper
  By Nukem9 (https://github.com/Nukem9/FaceFXWrapper), MIT license for its own code; it uses code from
  Bethesda's Creation Kit. Vault-Talk downloads it from its author's own release page.

FonixData.cdf
  Part of Bethesda Softworks' Creation Kit / G.E.C.K. (lip-sync data). Property of Bethesda Softworks LLC.
  Offered here unchanged, for players who don't have the editor installed.

xWMAEncode.exe
  Microsoft Corporation's xWMA encoder, from the DirectX SDK (June 2010). Property of Microsoft.

Vault Boy pictures
  The status faces in the launcher are Fallout: New Vegas' own message icons. Fallout, Vault Boy and
  Vault-Tec are trademarks of Bethesda Softworks LLC.

Pocket TTS, OmniVoice, omnivoice-server, PyTorch, faster-whisper, Python
  Installed, when the player asks, from their own official sources (python.org, pypi.org,
  download.pytorch.org, huggingface.co), under their own licenses.

Player2
  An optional AI service (https://player2.game). Vault-Talk connects to it with the player's own account.
